Welcome to the Reborn Region! The first thing you need to do is to fill out your name and character portrait. I recommend naming yourself "Leavanny". Most of the portraits have a bad case of Fetal Alcohol Syndrome (something that seems to plague Pokémon fangames).

Passwords

You'll board the train towards Reborn City through the Toumaline Desert, and you will be asked for "special instructions or passwords". Say YES here, because this is where you can enable certain QOL functionality that the game doesn't have enabled by default. There's a few passwords you absolutely should enable no matter what and a few that I recommend but can be enabled for personal taste.

Must-haves

  • easyhms - enables Gen 7 style HMs; that is, you don't need to have a Pokémon learn a HM to be able to use it outside of battle. I don't know why this isn't the default behaviour (good luck fighting bosses with a useless Bibarel in your party.)
  • hardcap - enables a hard level cap. Pokémon won't obey you beyond the level cap in Reborn; enabling this means that they simply won't level up past it until you get the next Gym badge.
  • weathermod - allows you to set the weather from the Pokégear instead of having to wait for it to change. This makes several events easier to get.
  • unrealtime - similar to above, lets you set the time.
  • antidote - disables poison damage when walking around in the overworld.
  • freeremotepc - gives you a Gen 8 style box link at the start of the game, instead of in the post-game. This can be a bit cheaty, but in the sections where it would truly break game galance (Elite 4) it's disaabled.
  • pinata - Free EXP candy from the trainers in the Grand Hall. This saves a lot of time compared to grinding them out. Not for true JRPG fans.

Good to have

  • freeexpall - Gives you Gen 7+ style EXP Share right away. This is useful for the immediate early-game before you get EXP Candies from the Grand Hall.
  • shinycharm - Reborn already boosts the shiny rate significantly; combining the rate boost of the shiny charm allows you to acquire shinies after five to ten minutes of grinding, which I believe is a fair time.
  • fullivs - Sets all your IVs to 31, so you don't have to grind bottle caps.
  • noitems and stopitems - Prevents both you and the AI from using items in-battle. This makes the game significantly harder but also makes gyms significantly more consistent and harder to cheese.
  • fieldnotes - You'll just look them up online anyway.
  • norolls - Removes damage rolls from the game, so every move does the same damage every time instead of slightly more or slightly less.

You can also enter any type name as a password, which will let you get some good Pokémon much earlier than normal, via early game events. You'll get an explanation of what events these are as you can access them. These passwords must must be entered during the starting sequence, as the event RNG is baked into the save as soon as you enter the Grand Hall.

I recommend you pick one of these codes instead of leaving it up to chance and getting an Elekid and a Ducklett from events.

  • monowoke - Porygon (starter), Bronzor (Slums), Staryu (Mystery), Zizagoon in Opal Ward
  • normal - Starly (Mystery)
  • fire - Vulpix/Larvesta/Litwick (Mystery)
  • water - Azurill/Mareanie/Staryu (Mystery), Ducklett (Slums)
  • grass - Shroomish/Cottonee (Mystery)
  • electric - Elekid (Mystery), Pachiritsu in Opal Ward (yikes!)
  • ice - Sneasel/Vulpix-A (Mystery), Cryogonal (Traded)
  • poison - Mareanie/Gastly (Mystery)
  • fighting - Shroomish (Mystery)
  • ground - Trapinch (Mystery)
  • flying - Starly/Togepi (Mystery), Ducklett (Slums)
  • bug - Larvesta (Mystery)
  • psychic - Staryu (Mystery)
  • rock - Rockruff (Mystery), Carbink (Traded)
  • ghost - Gastly/Litwick (Mystery)
  • dragon - Axew/Trapinch (Mystery)

Welcome To Reborn City

♫ Pokémon Reborn - Peridot Ward

{{ ss("peridot_1") }}

After entering all of your passwords, the train you're on will crash into the station shortly after you jump out through the window. You'll wake up... in the Peridot Ward of Reborn City.

You then meet your first leader - Julia - who talks like a baby about explosions. She asks you if you're challenging the Reborn League (yes) and then runs away.

Most of Lower Peridot Ward is blocked from you to stop you from walking up and getting into battles with no Pokémon. Talk to everyone here for some nice flavour text - you should be talking to absolutely everyone you see, anyway - and proceed to the right to make it to the Opal Ward.

{{ ss("shinycharm") }}

Talk to the purple-haired person at the bottom next to the river to get a single Potion. It doesn't work on humans, but she knows that! There are two staircases to either side, but they're blocked off by two trainers who won't let you past unless you have a Pokémon. If you used the early shiny charm password, there will be an item to the side of the middle staircases that contains it.

You have nowhere else to go but up the middle staircase and into the Grand Hall, where you will meet Victoria, your first rival! She'll take you inside the Grand Hall to the registration desk (hilariously, you walk a little bit faster than she does so this sequence looks pretty glitchy). And now it is time for.....

Choosing Your Starter
In the starter room, you will find a Sunkern Switcher. Ignore all of these. You must use the regular, non-All Gen teams as they are the ones that have been rebalanced. There is no guarantee that the All-Gen teams even exist in the exported files anymore!

Reborn is a hard game. Every major boss battle has a team that hits hard, and usually has a cheaty field effect that directly advantages their type and shuts down the most common counters.

The starter Pokémon are some of the best Pokémon available until the encounter tables open up around the third badge. All starters are also available via either the wild or events so you don't have to worry about missing any of them.

You can get away with any starter, some are very obviously better than others:

  • {{ sp("froakie") }} or {{ sp("scorbunny") }} are great choices overall due to their ability Protean/Libero which is one of the best abilities in the game. {{ sp("sprigatito") }} also has Protean, but it is weak to some of the most important first gyms; you can catch one at {{ mp(234) }} later on, where it becomes one of the best Pokémon available.
  • {{ sp("torchic") }} used to be the best starter hands down, but with the removal of Speed Boost it loses a lot of its previous advantage. You can get it via event near the end of the game.
  • Starter Porygon with the monowoke password, Starter Eeevee with the eevee password, or starter Alolatales with the vulpixpls password also make great choices.

Beyond that, the game relies very heavily on team composition; don't expect to coast along with an overlevelled starter like the main series games! You will need a good roster of Pokémon to tailor your teams to each boss. I recommend having around thirty to forty Pokémon by the last few gyms that you can rotate in and out as needed.

Grand Beginnings

Once you've picked your starter, you'll be led back downstairs and Victoria will go upstairs to pick her starter (which will always be a {{ sp("LITTEN")}}). Unfortunately, your second rival - Cain - will barge in, corner you, make inappropriate sexual remarks at you, and then force you into a battle. I think there should be an option to use your Pokémon to target him directly and kill him.

Once the battle ends, you will regain free movement and can explore the Opal Ward and Peridot Ward fully. If you used any of the item passwords, pick them up to the right of the Grand Hall.

At the very bottom of the Grand Hall, next to the exit, there are two NPCs. The one to the right will offer to sell you a rare Pokémon for only 500 dollarpounds; spoilers - it's a {{ sp("MAGIKARP") }}. This is a very long but optional event chain that leads you on until the very end of the game, and is the only way of actually catching a Magikarp in-game until the post-game (where you can breed {{ sp("GYARADOS") }} instead). If you do choose to buy it, it'll be stolen immediately. No refunds!

Opal Ward

Exit the Grand Hall to the south. Before you can go anywhere you're treated to a quick explanation about the soft level cap and disobedient Pokémon - the specific Pokémon varies depending on your save between either an {{ sp("STUFFUL") }} or an {{ sp("SNUBBULL") }}. Stufful isn't available again until Route 1 (via Bewear), and Snubbull isn't available again until after the 13th Badge.

{{ ss("softguestcap") }}

If you enabled the hard level cap, then Pokémon simply won't level past your level cap. You don't need to worry about using Common Candies to level your Pokémon back below your current level cap.

On both sides of the Grand Hall is a small wild area. There's nothing here really worth wasting your first set of Pokéballs on, but there is a single Green Shard available if you interact with the furthest left statue. Battle the single trainer, he tells you about Hidden Abilities and how they appear on wild Pokémon in Reborn; every Pokémon has up to three regular abilities instead.

If you try going up the staircases again, you will be again be blocked by the bridge guards - but this time, you can battle them! For the left stairs:

And then for the right stairs:

This bridge will slowly fill up with new NPCs after major story events who will want to battle you. Keep this in mind if you're transitting across Reborn City with injured Pokémon as they will gladly wipe you.

On the bridge itself are some battleable NPCs. There's also a set of guys bullying a Pokémon; this is either a {{ sp("ZIGZAGOON") }} or a {{ sp("PACHIRISU") }}. You can't defend it right now (if it's a Pachirisu you should let them throw it into the river) so make a note and move on.

To the north and the right of the bridge are two piggies who block your path into both parts of Obsidia Ward, so your only option is to go southeast into Lower Peridot Ward. The piggies that were blocking that part of the map are gone so you can explore the entirety of Peridot Ward. Just be warned; a lot of the random NPCs will pick fights with you here.

Upper Lower Peridot

Next, above the Pokécentre, there is a Doxy trainer who will battle you. Defeat her, and then talk to her again to get a Rose Incense. You can also interact with the rock below to get a single Antidote. The house to the left directly above has an event {{ sp("SKITTY") }}, although I can't particularly recommend you bother with that. You will be able to catch it in the wild after the 13th Badge.

To the right is a conspicious alleyway guarded by a single trainer. Defeat him to gain access to the alley; inside, you will find a Super Potion guarded by another trainer. This alley also has some very decent earlygame encounters, like {{ sp("TRUBBISH") }} and {{ sp("STUNKY") }}.

Even further to the right, just before the upper entrance to the Opal Ward, is a flat with a {{ sp("KRICKETOT") }} in it. I highly recommend you get this Pokémon, as Technician Kricketune can carry you really hard throughout the first few gyms.

A small area in Opal Ward is accessible to you by following the railway tracks to the east. It has a single trainer in it, and in rainy weather one of the elemental monkeys is available. If you keep going to the right, you will enter the Underground Railnet - or the very entrance. The rest is blocked off by a breakable rock.

If you have DEBUG enabled (like I do), then you can use all HMs at all times. This will break everything. If you debug-smash the rock, you will enter an area with level 30 Pokémon and eat shit. Use your common sense! Don't skip through HM-locked areas early!

Finally, to the left of the train tracks up to North Peridot Ward is a building with a flower field next to it. Go inside, beat up the old man, and you'll get access to the garden. The only thing worth catching here is a {{ sp("SWABLU") }} which has Cloud Nine, an ability useful for certain major story battles by cancelling out their field weather.

North Peridot Ward

{{ ss("charm") }}

Follow the train tracks up into North Peridot Ward. You will want to move further up in the ward to find a vendor that sells Pokésnax, which you can buy three of to get the rest of the event Pokémon. On the way, enter this house to get the TM for Charm. You can also go left into another alley, where you will find a Data Chip guarded by two other trainers.

If you didn't enter the right passwords in the introduction, you can use a data chip at any computer to enable or disable passwords. There's a small handful of these spread around the game. This means you can enable or disable litemode before certain story battles if you get hard stuck in the later parts of the game.

If you travel all the way north to the gate, on days with clear weather (or if you have weather mod enabled and set the weather to clear), you can use the Rose Incense you found earlier to catch a {{ sp("BUDEW") }}.

We're almost done with all of the available event Pokémon. If you have the weather mod, set the weather to Rainy or Stormy and interact with the fountain to the right of the railway to catch a {{ sp("SURSKIT") }}. Finally, set the time to night, go down to the left of the house with Charm in it, and enter the building to catch a {{ sp("JOLTIK") }} (provided you have a Pokésnax.)

{{ ss("pokesnax") }}

In clear weather, this NPC will sell Pokésnax for 650 dollarpounds letting you go back and get all of the event Pokémon that you missed. With that, you've finally caught all of the event Pokémon available to you! For now.

Mosswater Industrial

 {{ ss("incel_fern") }}

To advance the story, walk to the east of the Pokémart in North Peridot Ward and talk to the green-haired guy. He will talk to you to tell you to fuck off, letting the NPC that he was "interrogating" escape; then introduces himself as Fern (your third rival), talks about how he's an Alpha Male, and then forces you into a battle.

{{ trainer_battle_content("Fern", "Hotshot", 0) }}

After beating him into the ground, another piggie comes along and also tells you both to fuck off. Your next destination (after healing up) is the other factory building in North Peridot Ward that you probably passed on the way up: it's just off the train tracks above the area transition. Fern meets you there, Julia shows up to blow up the door and make another bad sex joke, Fern's sister (Pokémon Sheldon) shows up to talk about how it's a bad guy hide out. Just hold down text skip for this one. You can then enter the building for your first dungeon. It's a double battle dungeon with Fern as your partner!

{{ ss("blacksteam_1") }}

Your only possible route here is to the north, so enter through the doorway. As you proceed slightly upwards, you'll be presented with a cutscene with the Meteor Leader - Ace - talking about data deletion. Once again, hold down text skip here.

The "puzzle" here is to walk around and find screens with numbers on in order to fill in the code to the elevator door to your immediate right. In fact, you have to do this multiple times. There's no penalty for getting the code wrong so you can bruteforce it in a handful of minutes if you so desire. Otherwise, you will need to fight several opponents in a double battle; your team will be healed after every battle as is tradition for partner battles in the mainline games.

This is a good chance to explain Field Effects. Field Effects are special effects that happen to every single battle in a certain map. They buff certain moves (usually always to your opponents advantage), nerf certain other moves (nearly always counterplay to their type), and can change into other fields when using certain moves. You can think of them as a boss battle difficulty modifier. Their effects are explained in more detail inside Field Notes, which you can find throughout the game (or if you used the password, right from the start). As an example, Mosswater Industrial uses the Factory Field - one of the most common fields - that boosts Electric type attacks universally, boosts some Steel moves, and can change into Short-Circuit Field when some Ground-type moves are used.
  1. The first step is to your immediate right. Battle the trainers, interact with the green monitor to get the third digit of the code: 7.
  2. Going upwards (we're doing this clockwise), battle the trainers, interact with the right-sided monitors to get some lore, and then interact with the left-sided monitor to get the second digit of the code: 1.
  3. Next, walk across the bridge to the right. The first digit is available in a little cutout at the top: 3.
  4. Finally, go through the small gap to the right of there and go upwards to get the final digit: 9.
{{ ss("mosswater_2") }}

You can then cut back downwards to make it to the elevator door, where you enter the full code: 3179. Go into the elevator for a small set of cutscenes where the power is cut, you're offered a job, the power is restored, and Ace makes a tactical retreat. It's not immediately obvious here but the left, right, and bottom sides of this room are doorways to the catwalks you saw previously. Going in the second downwards exit leads you to a room with a single Small EXP Candy.

  1. First, walk out of the left exit and cross the platform all the way to the left. There will be another set of monitors in the room with the first digit of the code: 4. It should go without saying that all of these rooms have trainer battles in them.
  2. Return to the central platform and go to the south room; the monitor in this one will give you the second digit of the code: 8.
  3. Travel all the way to the right across three rooms to find another monitor. This gives you the last digit of the code: 2.
  4. Finally, return to the security room and talk to Florina, who will give you the third digit of the code: 6.

The code entrypoint is to the top of the central platform; the final code is then 4862. Enter it, then enter the room to get the explosives. Return back to Julia, who will prime the explosives.... and the door will open by itself. Good thing you didn't waste your time bruteforcing the code, eh? Go through the door and across the catwalk to confront Team Meteor. You then fight two named grunts with their own cut out - almost a boss battle! (But they only have two Pokémon vs your nine.)

At this point the EXP Candy trainer in front of the desk of the Grand Hall is unlocked. If you talk to them they will give you a varying number of EXP candies after either a battle (without the pinata password) or immediately (with the password). This means you can avoid grinding entirely and always have your team at the level cap. Here's a nice table for what candies they give out, and when:

Gym #1

♫ GlitchxCity - Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Sky Tower Remix

This gym is the easiest gym in the game by some margin. Don't get used to the difficulty.
{{ ss("julia_gym") }}

It's time to collect your first badge. Reborn's gyms vary a bit in quality, with some having real puzzles and others being a line of NPCs to kill before the main boss fight. Every gym also has an intercom at the start to talk to somebody from the League for fight advice, but this tends to range from "useless" to "actively deterimental".

The puzzle here is very simple; you simply lead the Voltorb in each indent up to the gates and interact with them to blow them up. (Damn, this gym must be expensive to run!) After blowing tthem all up, you can fight Julia in the uppermost room; I recommend saving with LTRIGGER before you talk to her as you can quick-reset with F12 if the battle goes downhill. She's a complete pushover and you should be able to roll over her with any non-Water starter; but if you're having trouble, here's a few other strategies you can try:

  • Technician {{ sp("KRICKETUNE") }} with Bug Buzz obliterates her, except for Oricorio.
  • Growth {{ sp("ROSELIA") }} can take most of her resisted hits and OHKO her back with +2 Magical Leafs, or +2 Mega Drains for healing. Again, keep Oricorio in mind.
  • If you used the mono Steel password, {{ sp("ARON") }} with Rock Throw and Metal Claw is super effective.

Her most dangerous Pokémon is the Oricorio which can set up Tailwind and dance all over you, especially if you picked a Grass starter. Make sure to eliminate it as soon as possible.

{{ trainer_battle_content("Julia", "JULIA", 0) }}

Julia will give you the Volt Badge, TM57 Charge Beam, the ability to use Cut outside of battle, and an increased level cap to Level 25. Before exiting the gym, set the weather to Stormy and go to the left with the radio dishes to find a wild {{ sp("BLITZLE") }}. We're almost done here in Peridot, but there's one missable event before we depart for South Obsidia.

Go back down to the riverside and talk to the hiker. He'll ask about places available to rent. Luckily... we do know one! It's the house where we previously found a Whismur. (If you didn't get the Whismur before, its now unavailable until Route 1 about twenty hours away.) Talk to the rent-seeker within; he's looking to let out the house to somebody. Go back to the hiker and tell him the good news. Finally, go back to the house and talk to the hiker again to get an {{ sp("ONIX") }}.

Now, you can make your way back into the Opal Ward and continue onwards to Obsidia Ward.
